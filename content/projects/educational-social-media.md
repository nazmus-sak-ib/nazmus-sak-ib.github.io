For our Social Communication course, we looked at how people in Bangladesh learn through Facebook and YouTube, and what makes educational content useful, accessible, and trustworthy.

I worked with **Tasnina Momo, Popy Akter, Nusrat Jahan Mousumi, and Namira Khondaker** on this report, submitted in September 2023.

## Our approach

We reviewed published work and examples of educational channels, looking at content formats, audiences, credibility, and the practical demands of creating material.

## What stood out

Views and engagement tell us little about whether people can use the material to learn. Our review considered language, access, credibility, and opportunities to ask questions, as well as the costs and platform incentives that affect whether creators can continue producing educational content.
