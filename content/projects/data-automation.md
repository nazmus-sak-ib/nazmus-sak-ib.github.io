Most of my freelance work started with something ordinary: a spreadsheet that needed too many manual steps, numbers that did not match, or information that had to move from one place to another.

*Before AI came and took my job, someone had to teach the spreadsheet to do the boring bits.*

[View my Upwork profile and client feedback](https://www.upwork.com/freelancers/~01ebb4624f51be9f6e?viewMode=1)

## Spreadsheet automation

I wrote and repaired Excel VBA macros and Google Apps Scripts for recurring spreadsheet tasks. These included listing and deleting records based on user input, processing records in chunks, checking day-to-day discrepancies, and converting an Excel VBA workflow to Google Apps Script.

## Connecting tools and producing reports

I connected APIs to Google Sheets, worked on API posting and script add-ins, generated reports from invoice data, and moved Excel information into Word templates. I also worked on spreadsheet forms and marking guides, and a Glide app template using Google Sheets or Airtable.

## Working through messy data

My projects included calculations on CSV files, data reconciliation, and custom data-processing workflows. Alongside the spreadsheet work, I took on Stata regression help and practical troubleshooting, including a Python installation on a Mac.
