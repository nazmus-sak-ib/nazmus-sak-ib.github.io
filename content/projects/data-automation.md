Most of my freelance work started with something ordinary: a spreadsheet that needed too many manual steps, numbers that did not match, or information that had to move from one place to another. The jobs varied in size, but they gave me plenty of practice turning loosely described requests into working tools.

*Before AI came and took my job, someone had to teach the spreadsheet to do the boring bits.*

[View my Upwork profile and client feedback](https://www.upwork.com/freelancers/~01ebb4624f51be9f6e?viewMode=1)

## Making spreadsheets do more of the work

I wrote and repaired Excel VBA macros and Google Apps Scripts for recurring spreadsheet tasks. These included listing and deleting records based on user input, processing records in chunks, checking day-to-day discrepancies, and converting an Excel VBA workflow to Google Apps Script.

Some jobs were small fixes. Others involved figuring out how a client's process should work before writing the script.

## Connecting tools and producing reports

I connected APIs to Google Sheets, worked on API posting and script add-ins, generated reports from invoice data, and moved Excel information into Word templates. I also worked on spreadsheet forms and marking guides, and a Glide app template using Google Sheets or Airtable.

The common thread was getting information into a useful form without asking someone to copy it around by hand.

## Working through messy data

My projects included calculations on CSV files, data reconciliation, and custom data-processing workflows. Alongside the spreadsheet work, I took on Stata regression help and practical troubleshooting, including a Python installation on a Mac.

That variety is part of why I like this work: sometimes the problem is statistical, sometimes it is a file format, and sometimes it is simply a broken macro standing between someone and their afternoon.

## How I worked with clients

I tried to establish what the output should look like before building it, then adjust the tool around how the client would actually use it. Client feedback on my profile mentions planning, communication, and availability for troubleshooting. The individual reviews and job history are linked above.
