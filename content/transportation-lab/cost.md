## What the study asked

How much does owning and operating a private car cost in Bangladesh, and how does that vary with monthly travel and whether a household employs a driver?

## Abstract synopsis

The study used a car-owner questionnaire to collect purchase price, registration, fuel, driver salaries, maintenance, insurance, taxes, parking, garage, and toll costs. It aimed to combine running expenses with ownership costs allocated over an assumed vehicle lifetime, allowing comparisons between households with and without drivers and between monthly and per-kilometer costs.

The working analysis explored how distance traveled and vehicle price related to expenses. It also raised questions about the reliability of self-reported travel distances and the sensitivity of results to depreciation and lifetime assumptions.
