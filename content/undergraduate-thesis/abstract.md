## Abstract synopsis

This undergraduate thesis examines intercity travel choices on the Dhaka–Cumilla route. An online questionnaire collected socioeconomic and trip information from 1,088 respondents. Buses accounted for approximately 93% of reported choices, and the subsequent analysis focused on air-conditioned and non-air-conditioned bus services.

A logit choice model examined how fare, in-vehicle travel time, income, and passenger characteristics related to service preference. The results suggested different relationships between price and choice across the two services and highlighted the potential relevance of perceived comfort and safety. Online sample selection and counterintuitive fare effects limit generalization and motivate further model development.
