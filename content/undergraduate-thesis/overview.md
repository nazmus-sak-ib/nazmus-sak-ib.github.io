## What we wanted to understand

Why does someone pay more for an air-conditioned bus, while someone else chooses the cheaper service? For my undergraduate thesis, we studied this question on the Dhaka–Cumilla route, looking at price, time, and the circumstances of the people making the trip.

## People behind the work

I completed the thesis with **Kamol Debnath Dip and Nishat Tasnim**, supervised by [Dr. M. Neaz Murshed](https://ce.buet.ac.bd/profile-of-m-neaz-murshed/) at BUET. It was accepted in February 2021.

## My thesis work

The work involved an online passenger survey, preparing and exploring the responses, comparing travel patterns, and developing a model of bus-service choice. This was a joint thesis; the document does not assign individual tasks to each author.

## What we found

Passengers weighed more than the fare alone. Travel time, income, and perceived riding quality helped explain the choices in our sample. Air-conditioned buses were rated more favorably on comfort and safety, but the online sample and some unexpected model results limited how broadly we could interpret those patterns.
